About

Talking Wilkins and Wontkins News Is a Scratch game that started development on March 20th, 2025.

It is a Talking tom and ben news parody. By the way, Talking Tom and Ben News belong to Outfit7

The game is made by NithikaD on scratch.mit.edu, and I also have a github account (Niki182) where I post updates about the game and maintain the website.
