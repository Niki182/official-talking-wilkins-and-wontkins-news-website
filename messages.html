PSA messages in this game

PSAs stands for Public Service Announcements

The PSA messages in this game are based on real life PSA messages, but they are not real PSA messages. They are just for fun and to add some humor to the game while being useful to your life at the same time.

Here are the PSA messages in this game and their meanings:

PSA Image or GIF Meaning
This is the first PSA message in the game. Never text and drive. It’s dangerous and can cause accidents. Stay focused on the road.
Never visit fake websites that try to trick you into giving away personal information.
Always wear your seatbelt when riding in a vehicle if it has one. It can save your life. This PSA is animated by the way.
Always be kind to others. Treat people with respect and empathy, both online and offline. Kindness makes the world a better place.
